Livin Digital ("we", "us") operates the Relax Crosswords mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account data: none. The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address or phone number.

Gameplay data: your puzzle progress, solved puzzles, hints balance, streaks, statistics and settings are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

AI puzzle themes: if you use the "Create an AI puzzle" feature, the theme text you type and your chosen grid size are sent to our server and to a third-party AI provider to generate the word list. Please do not enter personal information as a theme. Generated puzzles are stored on our server so they can be replayed.

Device and usage data: like most apps, the App and our service providers may automatically receive technical information such as device type, operating system, language, approximate (city-level) location derived from IP address, crash reports and general usage statistics.

2. Advertising

The App is free and supported by advertising served through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and app interaction data to show and measure ads, including interstitial ads and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch in exchange for hints.

Where required by law you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy › Tracking; Android: Settings › Google › Ads). Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate and improve the App, generate puzzles you request, provide a daily puzzle, display ads that keep the App free, diagnose crashes and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the App (advertising networks such as Google AdMob, AI generation and hosting providers), when required by law, or to protect our rights. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies.

5. Data retention & deletion

Local gameplay data stays on your device until you use Settings › Reset all progress or uninstall the App. AI-generated puzzle word lists stored on our server are not linked to you personally. You may contact us at any time to request deletion of information you believe relates to you.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR or California under CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising. To exercise these rights contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will change and, for material changes, we will provide notice within the App.
